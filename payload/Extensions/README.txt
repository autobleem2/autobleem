AutoBleem extensions
====================

Extensions add screens and services to the launcher itself - the AutoBleem Store is one.
Each extension is a folder of its own, unpacked here by hand:

    Extensions/<name>/
        extension.ini
        bin/<platform>/<name>.so        (.dll on Windows)

The System menu (L2+R2) -> Extensions lists what is installed; Cross runs one, Triangle
switches it on or off. An extension is installed and updated by hand only - nothing,
not even the Store, installs one for you.
